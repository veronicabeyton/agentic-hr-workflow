# COMPANY HR SALARY ADVANCE & EXPENSE RECONCILIATION POLICY

1. RECEIPT DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
- Employees may submit between 1 and 3 receipts per report.
- Receipts #2 and #3 are OPTIONAL. If fields for Receipt #2 or #3 are left blank, IGNORE them entirely—do NOT flag the submission as missing data.
- For every receipt actually submitted, all four fields must be filled:
  1. Receipt Date
  2. Company / Vendor Name
  3. Official Receipt or Invoice Number
  4. Total Amount Paid
- If a submitted receipt has partial information (e.g., employee entered Receipt #1 Date and Amount, but left Receipt #1 Number blank), flag it for human review.

2. TIMELINESS & DATE VALIDITY
- All receipts must be dated within 30 days of the expense report submission date.
- Future-dated receipts are strictly invalid and must be flagged as non-compliant.

3. FINANCIAL RECONCILIATION RULES
- Total Expense Amount = Receipt 1 Amount + Receipt 2 Amount + Receipt 3 Amount.
- Variance Calculation = (Total Approved Receipts) - (Initial Advance Provided).
  * If Variance = 0: Advance is fully reconciled and settled.
  * If Variance < 0 (Receipts < Advance): Employee owes the balance back to the company.
  * If Variance > 0 (Receipts > Advance): Company owes reimbursement to the employee (requires manager re-approval if variance exceeds $100).

4. AUDIT COMPLIANCE STATUS CODES
- APPROVED: All receipt data points are valid, math reconciles correctly, and no policy rules are violated.
- REJECTED: Contains prohibited expense categories, invalid future dates, or fake/duplicate receipt numbers.
- NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW: Missing receipt numbers/vendor names, date older than 30 days, or receipts do not sum to the declared advance amount.
